for 문

${cb(`for i in range(5): print(i, "번째 반복")`)}

range() 활용

${cb(`for i in range(1, 11): # 1~10 print(i, end=" ") for i in range(0, 11, 2): # 0,2,4...10 print(i, end=" ")`)}

while 문

${cb(`count = 1 while count <= 5: print(count, "번째") count += 1`)}
